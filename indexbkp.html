Dhikala Corbett

Dhikala guest houses are the unique mode of accommodation amidst the dense forest of Corbett National Park . To reach Dhikala rest house arrive at Dhangari Gate, which will take you through the dense Sal forest of Corbett. Dhikala is 30 km away from the entrance and it takes nearly 2 hours to reach.
