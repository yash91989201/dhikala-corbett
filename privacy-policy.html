Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 27 July 2026

The Indian Voyage, a premier travel brand owned and operated by Green View Voyage Pvt. Ltd. (CIN: U63040DL2010PTC201816), is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency in the collection, use, and protection of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and disclose your personal information when you visit https://www.dhikala-corbett.com , submit an enquiry to book forest rest house package through our website.

  1. Information We Collect

    To provide our Forest Rest House package and travel consultancy services, we need collect the following information for each member travelling in group

    Personal Information

    • Full Name
    • Age
    • Gender
    • Country of Residence
    • Email Address
    • Mobile/Phone Number

    Identity Verification Information

    For processing your forest rest house package we need to book your night stay permit for forest rest house for that we need to collect:

    • Government-issued ID Type (such as Aadhaar Card, Passport, Driving Licence, Voter ID, etc.)

    • Number of Travellers (adults, children)

    • Number of extra bed required
    • Any additional information voluntarily provided during your enquiry or booking.

  2. How We Use Your Information

    Your information is collected solely for Forest Rest House night stay permit booking including:

    • Responding to your enquiries.
    • Preparing travel quotations and itineraries.
    • Processing bookings for Forest Rest Houses.
    • Allotment of jeep for package
    • Obtaining night stay permits from Corbett Tiger Reserve Website
    • Sending booking confirmations, travel updates, invoices, and other essential communications.
    • Sharing guest contact detail with our ground team
    • Processing refunds, where applicable.
    • Maintaining internal business records and improving our customer service.
    • Complying with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

  3. Data Sharing and Operational Transparency

    To complete your booking, we share your personal information only with the third parties necessary to provide the requested services.

    • Name
    • Age
    • Gender
    • ID type and ID number

    This information is shared directly with the official Corbett Tiger Reserve booking website (corbettgov.org) to obtain your night stay permit, which is an essential requirement for the Forest Rest House package. To learn how your information is handled by them, please review the official Corbett Tiger Reserve Privacy Policy .

We retain this information only for as long as necessary to process the refund and comply with applicable legal, accounting, and regulatory obligations.

We also do not send unsolicited promotional emails, WhatsApp messages, SMS messages, or telephone marketing communications. Our communications are limited to responding to your enquiry, processing your booking, and providing essential travel-related updates.

You may disable cookies through your browser settings; however, certain website features may not function correctly if cookies are disabled.
