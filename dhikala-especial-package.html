Dhikala Especial Package


Duration: 2 Nights / 3 Days

Day 1 : Dhikala Rest House in Dhikala zone- Corbett National Park (Inside Stay)

Arrival at Corbett national park at 11am. From here board 4x4 jeep and start your journey inside the jungle via Dhangari gate of Corbett national park. Dhikala zone is famous among wildlife photographers and nature lovers for its stunning beauty and variety of fauna and flora. During this journey you will pass through the dense sal forests and grasslands inhabiting wide range of wildlife at Corbett national park. Lunch at rest house. In the afternoon go for jeep safari around Dhikala zone of Corbett National Park. Dinner and overnight stay at forest rest house.

Day 2: Corbett National Park-Outside stay(Camp Riverwild/Similar)

Early morning jeep safari of dhikala zone. After safari return to rest house for breakfast. Relax for few hours between breakfast and lunch at rest house. After lunch in afternoon again proceed for jeeps safari. After safari between 04:00pm to 05:00pm come out of dhikala zone and reach to your private resort. Check in room at resort. Dinner and overnight at resort.

Check Availability
Day 3: Depart Corbett National Park:

After breakfast depart from Corbett National Park.
