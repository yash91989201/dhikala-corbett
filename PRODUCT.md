## Users

Wildlife travellers planning a stay inside Corbett Tiger Reserve: Indian families and couples, photographers, and corporate/group organisers; inbound foreign travellers comparing Dhikala zone stays and safaris are a first-class audience. Their job is to confirm real forest-rest-house availability, understand permit and safari rules, and book a stay-plus-safari trip without calling the forest department themselves. *(Audience ranking inferred from existing site copy, testimonial mix, and enquiry flow.)*

## Product Purpose

Dhikala-Corbett.com is a specialist guide and booking portal for Dhikala Forest Rest House and the Dhikala zone of Jim Corbett National Park. It explains the zone, its five forest rest houses, safari modes, and access rules, then converts that knowledge into enquiry-led bookings. Success is a confirmed, correctly permitted stay-and-safari booking and a guest who returns or refers.

## Positioning

Deep, zone-specific operational knowledge plus authorised inventory: the operator is a booking and marketing partner / channel manager for forest rest houses and partner resorts, runs its own shortlisted guides and drivers, and has 15+ years on the ground. A generic travel aggregator cannot truthfully copy the dated-permit and FRH-rule expertise.

## Operating Context

Bookings are enquiry-driven (web forms, phone, WhatsApp), not instant checkout. Dhikala access is through Dhangarhi Gate; jeep safari inside the zone requires a stay at a zone forest rest house; canter safari is the alternative. Inventory is released by the forest department and is date-bound, so availability, cancellation, and ID rules dominate the visitor's questions. The site is a static HTML/Bootstrap/jQuery build with PHP form endpoints, deployed over FTP.
